# Drive answers to the four authentication seeks.
# Seek 1's word must be within 18 of seek 0's, and seek 3's within 20 of seek 2's.
# Seeks 2 and 3 also carry report bytes a b c d: b must be at least a / 2, d at least c / 2.
# A failed check ends with 3. The next timer command starts a new check.
#
#   python ring.py cdb105.bin seeks.txt

# Answers at the limits: 18 and 20 apart, each second byte exactly half the first.
timer 0145
seek 1000
seek 1012
seek 2000 61 30 81 40
seek 1fec 61 30 81 40

# The answer Yabause's drive emulation gives to every ring seek.
timer 0145
seek 44f1 09 09 09 09
seek 44f1 09 09 09 09
seek 44f1 09 09 09 09
seek 44f1 09 09 09 09

# A word of 0 or ffff makes the drive seek again 206 frames further on.
timer 0145
seek 0000
seek 1000
seek ffff
seek 1010
seek 2000 40 20 40 20
seek 2008 40 20 40 20

# Seek 1's word is 19 away from seek 0's.
timer 0145
seek 1000
seek 1013

# The distance does not wrap around: 0004 and 7ff0 are 32,748 apart.
timer 0145
seek 0004
seek 7ff0

# A kept word of 8000 or above is read back sign-extended, which fails the check whatever word
# follows: fff0 and 0004 are 4,294,967,276 apart.
timer 0145
seek fff0
seek 0004

# Seek 2's byte b is below a / 2.
timer 0145
seek 1000
seek 1000
seek 2000 61 2f 81 40

# Three answers of 0 or ffff for one seek: the drive gives up.
timer 0145
seek 1000
seek 0000
seek ffff
seek 0000
